02 / About Anushka Singh

A coach who has worked the sport from every side.

Anushka Singh is a yoga teacher and trainer, and founder of Raj Yogasana Academy in Etah. She has competitive Yogasana experience, is a State Yogasana medalist, and has served as a technical official and judge.

She coached the Women's Team of Uttar Pradesh at the Senior & Senior A National Yogasana Championship, has supported national and international Yogasana events, and is a core organizing team member of the 1st World Yogasana Sports Championship 2026 in Ahmedabad, India.

Students: adults, beginners, children, athletes, and learners online in India and abroad.
